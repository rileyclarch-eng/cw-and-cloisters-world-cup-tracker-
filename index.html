    Tie-break rules

    Survival order

    1. Compare everyone's best remaining team.
    2. If that is tied, compare second-best team.
    3. Keep going through third, fourth and fifth teams.
    4. No points scoring, no bonus rules, no maths degree required.

    Simple example

    If Claire and David both have a team reach the final, compare their next-best teams. If Claire's second team lasted longer, Claire sits above David.
